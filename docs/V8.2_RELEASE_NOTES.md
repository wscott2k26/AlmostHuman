# Almost Human 8.2 — Competitive Master Pass

Version 8.2 expands the verified V8.1 foundation into a fuller AI life-sim and memory product while preserving the original digital-birth premise and user-control guardrails.

## Added

- Optional private daily mood check-ins with no streak, penalty, or dependency language.
- Stage-aware conversation sparks and memory-based follow-up prompts.
- Visible interest affinity, skill proficiency, and relationship compass.
- Automatic life journal combining milestones, activities, check-ins, and memories.
- On-this-day resurfacing and future letters that unlock by simulated age.
- Earned keepsake room using existing developmental room unlocks.
- Visual keepsake gallery for created media.
- Stronger liquid-glass material depth, tactile press compression, and supported device haptics.
- Reduced-motion, reduced-transparency, high-contrast, and responsive fallbacks.

## Competitive guardrails

The pass translates principles from leading AI companion, virtual-pet, and journal products without copying proprietary layouts, avatars, copy, shops, screenshots, or navigation. Almost Human avoids romance-first defaults, discovery feeds, streak shame, randomized loot, engagement guilt, and interruption paywalls.
