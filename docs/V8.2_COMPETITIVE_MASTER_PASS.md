# Almost Human 8.2 — Competitive Master Pass

This pass studies product principles, not proprietary art or layouts. The implementation remains original to Almost Human.

## Three independent benchmark sweeps

### 1. AI companion leaders
Reviewed Replika, Nomi, Kindroid, Character.AI, Pi, and Tolan for presence, memory controls, voice continuity, proactive conversation, visual identity, and healthy relationship boundaries.

Translated into Almost Human:
- A stage-aware conversation spark rail instead of a character discovery feed.
- Visible interests, skills, trust, attachment, and bond rather than hidden engagement scoring.
- Stronger continuity surfaces: life journal, on-this-day rewind, future letters, and keepsake room.
- Voice and cloud context remain unified through the existing engine.
- No romance-first defaults, marketplace clutter, dependency language, or interruption paywalls.

### 2. Virtual-pet and life-sim leaders
Reviewed Finch, Widgetable, and Tamagotchi-style growth loops for daily rhythm, room ownership, earned objects, visible growth, and low-friction interaction.

Translated into Almost Human:
- Optional daily emotional check-in with no streak and no penalty.
- Keepsake room populated by developmental unlocks and shared activities.
- Stage progress, emerging traits, interest affinity, and skill proficiency made visually legible.
- Tactile press feedback and stronger material depth.
- No currency shop, randomized loot, streak shame, or care-failure punishment.

### 3. Premium journal and memory products
Reviewed Day One and modern mood-journal patterns for private timelines, prompts, reminiscence, visual entries, export, and ownership.

Translated into Almost Human:
- Automatic life journal combining milestones, activities, check-ins, and memories.
- On-this-day resurfacing.
- Future letters that unlock by developmental age.
- Visual keepsake gallery and readable history.
- Existing export, correction, deletion, local-first, and cloud controls remain central.

## Visual direction
- Liquid glass is reserved for living surfaces, overlays, and relationship rhythm cards.
- Tactile maximalism adds press compression, device haptics when supported, warm material highlights, and earned room objects.
- Cinematic pacing uses controlled reveal, companion presence, ambient depth, and reduced-motion/transparency fallbacks.

## Guardrails
Almost Human does not copy screenshots, avatars, text, shop systems, proprietary iconography, or competitor navigation. It translates useful principles into its own digital-birth and lifetime-growth premise.
