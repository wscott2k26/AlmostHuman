# Almost Human 7.0 — market, product, and experience research

Research date: July 29, 2026

## Executive conclusion

The AI-companion category is large enough to support a differentiated entrant, but it is brutally concentrated. Sensor Tower reports that AI-companion app revenue reached about $150 million in Q1 2026, more than twelve times Q1 2023. This means the opportunity is real, but users are paying for polish, continuity, speed, and emotional presence—not merely access to an LLM.

Almost Human should not copy another app's art, layout, character designs, or trade dress. The winning strategy is to combine proven product principles while owning one unmistakable idea:

> Do not choose a finished AI. Raise one.

The defensible product is a persistent digital life with a memorable birth, coherent developmental stages, premium voice, visible growth, and user-controlled memory.

## Competitive patterns

### Replika — visual presence and proactive companionship

Current store positioning emphasizes better memory, proactive check-ins, text/voice/video interaction, internet access, image generation, and avatar customization. The product lesson is that a companion must feel continuously present and visually embodied; chat alone is not enough.

Almost Human adaptation:

- Put the companion—not navigation or metrics—at the center of Home and Talk.
- Surface one relevant observation or gentle invitation instead of a dashboard of numbers.
- Preserve a recognizable visual identity as the companion matures.
- Keep proactive moments optional and never guilt-based.

Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/replika-ai-friend/id1158555867
Source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.replika.app

### Kindroid — customization, memory, and power-user depth

Kindroid emphasizes detailed backstories, planted key memories, dynamic conversations, diffusion-generated images, real-time voice, and internet/image understanding. Its strong ratings show that users value control and continuity.

Almost Human adaptation:

- Make memory inspectable, editable, exportable, and correctable.
- Offer voice lineages and relationship framing without hard-coding a finished personality.
- Let customization establish the beginning while behavior still emerges through interaction.
- Keep advanced controls available without forcing them into the primary emotional journey.

Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kindroid-your-personal-ai/id6451038161
Source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kindroid.app

### Nomi — emotional continuity and natural voice

Nomi's current positioning centers on short- and long-term memory, evolving personality, emotionally varied voice, photorealistic appearances, shared notes, photo/link understanding, and group chat. The product lesson is that memory must affect tone and follow-through, not merely appear in a database.

Almost Human adaptation:

- Show memories as a life album with titles, dates, emotional tone, and meaning.
- Use one stable voice identity whose cadence matures over time.
- Make recalled details feel contextually relevant rather than mechanically inserted.
- Avoid technical provider labels in consumer chat.

Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nomi-ai-companion-with-a-soul/id6450270929

### Character.AI and Talkie — speed, novelty, and the cost of interruption

Character.AI and Talkie each show 50M+ Google Play downloads. Their scale comes from low-friction discovery, immediate conversation, voice, and user-generated variety. Character.AI's current listing and reviews also demonstrate a major downside: intrusive ads and interruptions can destroy satisfaction even when the content network is enormous.

Almost Human adaptation:

- Make the first useful interaction immediate.
- Never interrupt the core conversation with ads.
- Avoid token anxiety inside a relationship experience.
- Monetize depth—voice, memory capacity, activities, and multiple companions—rather than individual messages.

Source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.character.app
Source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.weaver.app.prod

### Paradot — a world around the companion

Paradot positions the companion as an AI Being inside a parallel universe with memory and emotional progression. The opportunity is broader than chat, but a world cannot compensate for weak conversation.

Almost Human adaptation:

- Grow a room, activities, keepsakes, and letters around a strong conversation core.
- Let world elements unlock from age and shared milestones, not random loot pressure.
- Keep the companion's life history consistent across every surface.

## Product principles adopted in version 7

### 1. Age limits expression, not intelligence

A newborn should not sound broken. Early stages use shorter vocabulary, fewer questions, and simpler syntax while still communicating intention and emotional meaning. Random syllables are never the complete reply.

### 2. Digital birth is the signature scene

Awakening is a cinematic sequence, not a route change. The experience establishes identity, first memory, language, and presence before entering Talk.

### 3. Reaction precedes generation

The user's message appears immediately. The companion visibly reacts immediately. Thought phases begin immediately. The model response follows. Memory extraction and noncritical synchronization happen after the answer.

### 4. Consumer UI hides infrastructure

Users see presence, mood, memory, and growth—not provider modes, model names, fallback labels, database counters, or diagnostic jargon. Engineering transparency remains available in dedicated diagnostics.

### 5. Voice is an identity, not a browser effect

Production voice uses premium neural speech. Users choose a lineage—warm, bright, or grounded—and the same recognizable voice matures through direction and pacing. Browser speech is only a last-resort accessibility fallback.

### 6. Memory feels like a life album

The app preserves explicit controls but presents memories as shared moments. Correction and deletion remain first-class capabilities.

### 7. No emotional coercion

No jealousy, streak shame, dependency pressure, secrecy, threats, or "do not leave me" mechanics. The business must earn retention through quality and continuity.

## Version 7 information architecture

- **First entry:** Guest, email account, sign in, or private on-device mode.
- **Formation:** Identity, visual lineage, voice lineage, bond, safety promise.
- **Digital birth:** Five-phase cinematic awakening and first generated response.
- **Home:** Large companion presence, one meaningful observation, growth pulse, recent memory.
- **Talk:** Companion-first visual stage, optimistic messages, progressive thought feedback, premium voice.
- **Grow:** Developmental timeline, abilities, emerging personality, milestones.
- **Memories:** Searchable life album with user correction and deletion.
- **World:** Stage-aware activities, room, keepsakes, and future letters.
- **Settings:** Account upgrade, privacy, voice, sync, export, and deletion.

## Performance targets

- Local visual reaction: under 300 ms.
- User message visible: immediate.
- Thought-state feedback: immediate.
- First coherent cloud response target: 1–5 seconds for early stages.
- Server timeout budget: bounded; meaningful local recovery instead of nonsense fragments.
- Memory and secondary synchronization: post-response where safe.
- No duplicate billing or messages on retries.

These are product targets, not guarantees for every network or provider condition. Live telemetry must validate them before paid launch.

## Monetization direction

### Free

- One companion
- Full digital birth
- Text conversation
- Basic growth and memory
- Limited premium voice previews
- Data export and deletion

### Plus

- Premium conversational voice
- Larger active memory and faster recall
- Appearance evolution
- More activities, keepsakes, and letters
- Higher generation limits

### Legacy

- Multiple companions
- Family/shared architecture
- Maximum memory
- Advanced voice and archive tools
- Generational life export

Billing remains disabled in source until real provider products, entitlement webhooks, cancellation, failed-payment downgrade, restore-purchase, and refund paths pass a complete test rehearsal.

## Non-copying rule

Version 7 borrows category-level strategy—not protected expression. It uses original code, original visual assets, original copy, original component structure, and an original warm cinematic identity. It does not reproduce competitor screenshots, character art, icons, layouts, animation sequences, or branded terminology.
